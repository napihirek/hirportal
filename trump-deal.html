Trump hails 'historic' deal for US to control 65bn barrels of Venezuela's oil

World News & Politics | Source: BBC News | Updated Just Now

The US has reached a landmark deal with Venezuela to control more than 65 billion barrels of its proven oil reserves, President Donald Trump announced today.

In a major policy speech, President Trump hailed the agreement as "historic," claiming it will reshape global energy markets and guarantee long-term stability across North and South America.

Under the terms of the agreement, international crude supply networks will undergo immediate restructuring, sending economic ripples through global oil markets and trade alliances...
