China Hits Out at 'Illegal' New US Sanctions on Iran and International Trading Partners

Geopolitics & World Economy | Source: BBC News | Updated 10 mins ago

Beijing has strongly condemned the latest round of unilateral sanctions imposed by Washington, targeted at entities conducting trade with Iran. Chinese officials warned that these restrictions severely violate international economic norms.

The decision escalates diplomatic tensions as Washington aims to choke off revenue streams tied to Iranian oil exports, while China vows to defend the legitimate rights and interests of its national enterprises.

"Beijing maintains that unilateral sanctions lacking UN backing are unlawful and threaten global supply chain stability."

International analysts predict that secondary sanctions on foreign financial institutions dealing with Iran could trigger further retaliation from Beijing, impacting global energy markets and trade shipping routes.
